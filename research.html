Graduate Research Assistant for Dr. Harvey Cutler

April 2019 – March 2026

Constructed computable general equilibrium (CGE) models and conducted studies on:

  • The economic benefits of improving wind speed forecasts.
  • The economic impacts of natural disasters on multiple regions.

  • The economic impact of reducing oil and natural gas production in Colorado.
